Privacy policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Who uses this app

This app is a personal tool. Only the site owner uses it, with their own Google account.

What it can access

The app asks Google for the drive.file permission only. This means it can see and change only the files that the app itself creates in Google Drive. It cannot see any other files in the Google Drive.

What it does with your data

  • It uploads video files from the owner's private server into the owner's Google Drive.
  • It does not collect, sell or share any personal information.
  • It does not send Google Drive data to anyone else.
  • The Google sign-in details are stored only on the owner's private server.

How to remove access

You can remove this app's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

For questions, use the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen for this app.
